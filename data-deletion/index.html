Privacy policy

ZippyScale (AMB Agency), Hyderabad, India. Last updated 17 October 2026.

This page explains what ZippyScale does with the information you share when you message us on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook Messenger, or fill in one of our lead forms on Facebook or Instagram.

What we collect

  • Your name and the account or number you wrote from.
  • The messages you send us, and our replies.
  • What you type into a lead form: usually your name, email, phone number, company and the question you asked.
  • Which advertisement brought you to us, so we know which ones are useful.

Why we use it

Only to answer you, to follow up on what you asked about, and to keep a record of our conversation with you. We do not sell it, rent it or share it with anybody for their own marketing.

Where it is kept

In ZippyScale's own working system, hosted with Cloudflare. Only ZippyScale staff who handle your enquiry can read it. Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook) also holds your messages under its own policy.

How long we keep it

For as long as we are in conversation or working together, and for up to two years after that for our records, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete all we hold about you at any time:

  1. Email bhargav@zippyscale.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us the name, phone number or Instagram or Facebook account you wrote to us from.
  3. We delete your messages, your lead form answers and your contact record within 30 days and write back to confirm it is done.

You can also remove ZippyScale's access from your Facebook or Instagram settings under Apps and Websites; that stops anything new reaching us.
