02// Design systemTwo fonts, nine palettes

Two typefaces. Nine palettes.

Every colour scheme is kept exactly as approved. What is new is the type and the language: one engineered sans and one mono, everywhere, and a technical layer of strips, glass and glow that each palette wears in its own colour.

Text / Geist variable / OFL
Aa Prathima
Light 290Book 380Display 473Medium 560

Every headline, paragraph and button. Headlines at weight 473, tracking minus 5 percent; body at 15 to 18px.

Labels and numbers / JetBrains Mono / OFL

13// QuestionsBefore we build

Seven questions to settle.

Answers change the build, not the look. Write a note under any page above, then press Copy my notes.

  1. What is the site mainly for: the group's brand and investor showcase, or leads for each business (appointments, admissions, bookings, tenders)? Our default: both, with one clear enquiry per business.
  2. Who owns and updates content for each business? One person per business, or one central marketing team?
  3. One site with a section per business, or separate sites on sub-domains? Our default: one site, short sub-domain links.
  4. Facts to confirm: Prathima Relief, Prathima Solar and Elgen, Columbian assisted living, Pristine Estates (we read "Prestient" on the menu), Tellapur Technocity.
  5. Claims to confirm: "#1 on TripAdvisor in Karimnagar", the aircraft fleet, PIMS programmes and seats, Foundation impact numbers.
  6. Photography: can we shoot each business (hospital, campus, sites, hotel, aircraft)? Hospitality, Aviation and Living have no photos of their own yet.
  7. Brand files: the lotus logo as a vector, and any brand colour guide.
