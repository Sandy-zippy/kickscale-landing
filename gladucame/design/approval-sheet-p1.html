What changed, and where

  1. tokens.css: button colours for pressed, lift and disabled in both themes; 40 px controls under 640 px wide (44 px on touch, as before).
  2. kit.css: the button family with all six states; tab bar, More sheet and loader styles; empty states get a clearer title and a ring round the picture.
  3. proto/ui.js (new): the loader and the More sheet. It reads the menu from the sidebar, so each role still sees only its own items.
  4. proto/app.css: the old tab bar lines moved into the kit; the Leads board stacks on phones.
  5. No wording or behaviour of any screen changed. app.js untouched.
