For Cockpit (the demo builder). Build every screen from these classes, as they are.
Do not edit anything in design/; ask Picasso. A change lands in the kit once and reaches every screen.

=====================================================================
2. HARD RULES
=====================================================================
 1. Colour only through tokens. No hex in web/ CSS. Never test [data-theme] in web/ CSS; if a screen
    needs a per-theme difference, ask Picasso for a token.
 2. MINT IS THE ACCENT, NOT A WALL. One .btn--next per screen region (the one obvious next action).
    Never a mint page, mint header band or mint card ground. Mint words use --accent-ink, never raw mint.
 3. Overdue = red, with a word ("Due yesterday", "Overdue 2 days"). Blocked and Snoozed = amber, with a
    word. Never colour alone.
 4. Every dialog has the Speak button (.mic with data-mic) in .dialog-head. No exceptions.
 5. Every list screen has the filter bar (.filters): search, dropdowns, "+ Any field", Date filter with
    Custom dates. Bulk lists (leads, influencers, journalists) add "Excel sample" + "Upload Excel".
 6. Every record page opens with .rec-sentence, ONE .btn--next, and "Step X of Y" (.steps + .steps-txt).
 7. Every mutating action answers with GUC.toast(text, undoFn). No confirm(), alert() or prompt().
 8. "New version, Reload now" notice (.notice) on every build: compare build stamp, show it, data-reload.
 9. Words: the BRIEF's list only (brand, lead, influencer, celebrity, journalist / outlet, hamper,
    coverage, live link, task, Today's tasks, Blocked, team thread, morning brief, department head,
    payout, Date filter, Dropdown lists, Speak). Also in toasts, bell text, History lines.
10. No em dashes anywhere. Dates print "26 Oct", times "3:30 pm". Money in ₹ with Indian grouping
    (₹8,00,000) or short form (₹8.0L) on tiles and cards.
11. Scope descendant selectors with ">" (a broad "li > span" once stretched every avatar).
12. Icons: <svg class="i" aria-hidden="true"><use href="design/icons.svg#i-bell"/></svg>.
    Icon-only buttons carry aria-label. Navigation items always show the word.

=====================================================================
3. THEME
=====================================================================
Two themes: light (DEFAULT) and dark. Saved per device in localStorage "guc.theme".
prefers-color-scheme is not read (the demo should open the same for everyone).
  GUCTheme.set('dark') . GUCTheme.get() . GUCTheme.toggle()
  Any element with data-theme-toggle flips it (one delegated listener; label child [data-theme-label]).
  Event 'guc:theme' on window after every change.
Tokens are also scoped to [data-theme], so <div data-theme="dark"> re-skins only its subtree.
