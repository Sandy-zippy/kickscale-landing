Your website is being blocked

Airtel is showing your customers a scam warning instead of your cars

On Airtel broadband and mobile, carcartonline.com does not load. Airtel's DNS redirects it to an “Airtel found this site dangerous” page that says the site was blocked to protect the visitor from a suspected online scam. A large share of Hyderabad is on Airtel, and every one of them who taps your link sees that instead of ₹20.82 Cr of inventory.

Your SSL certificate is completely valid — it expires 16 October 2026 and is correctly issued. This is not a security fault on your site. It is a reputation flag on the domain, and it is fixable.

  1. Write to spam.grievance@airtel.com from your company email, asking for a review and delisting of carcartonline.com.
  2. Give them the domain, your business name, GST number and showroom address so they can confirm it is a real business.
  3. Check the same domain against Google Safe Browsing and the other ISP blocklists at the same time.
  4. Once cleared, keep the domain clean — no cracked plugins, no unknown redirects.

We will draft the delisting request for you.

Errors we found in your listings

CC-318Range Rover Sport — the make is recorded as “RANGE ROVER”, a separate make from Land Rover, so anyone filtering for Land Rover never sees this car. Its fuel type is spelt “DEISEL”, so it also disappears from every diesel search.
CC-390Toyota Hilux — the make is recorded as “HILUX” and the model as “HIGH”, so it shows on your site as a Toyota called “High” and never appears under Toyota.
CC-358Mercedes G 63 AMG, your most expensive car at ₹3.80 Cr — it has no kilometre reading at all. That is the first question every buyer asks.
CC-2019Skoda Superb — the year 2019 has been typed into the stock-number field, so the car has no real stock number.

Beyond these four, the bigger issue is what is simply not recorded. Across all 25 cars there is no owner count, no service history, no insurance status, no warranty, no tyre condition, no emission norm, no power or torque figure, no EMI, and no availability status — and not one video or written description. That is why your listings cannot answer a buyer's questions, and why customers phone to ask things a website should have told them.

Your own inventory also has more cars on CarWale than on your website. The marketplace you pay for is carrying stock your own site has never heard of.
